My Approach

I believe effective AI governance must be practical, risk-driven, evidence-based, and proportionate to the potential impact.

I focus on identifying meaningful risks, evaluating controls, documenting evidence, prioritizing remediation, and ensuring accountability.

AI should support decision-making, not replace authorized human judgment for consequential decisions or risk acceptance.
